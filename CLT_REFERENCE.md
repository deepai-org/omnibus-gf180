# Critical Loop Theory — user-proposed postulates

Authority: the project author's definition supplied in this conversation, 2026-10-25. This is an original theory proposal, not an established result or an external published standard. The text below is preserved as supplied. `Phi_CLT` and `Phi_IIT` refer to different quantities and shall not be interchanged.

## Source text

**P1 (Ontology — identification, not addition).** Experience is identical to a physical process: information circulating through irreducible closed causal loops. No new field, force, or coupling constant; the relation is like temperature = mean kinetic energy. (Satisfies 17; the near-exclusion of any new action term by locality and precision data makes this reading almost forced.)

**P2 (The quantity).** For a subsystem S in state ρ over window T, define Φ[S,T] as the weighted sum over closed cycles of dressed retarded response functions of the coarse-grained neural/activity field theory (Schwinger–Keldysh/MSR formalism), weighted by information actually transported per cycle. Counterfactual causal structure thereby becomes a measurable correlator, not a metaphysical extra.

**P3 (Boundaries and scale).** The conscious entity is the partition and coarse-graining scale that locally extremize Φ, with irreducibility enforced by subtracting the best factorized decomposition.

**P4 (Time).** Φ is defined only over windows comparable to loop transit times; experience is intrinsically extended (~10²–10³ ms), never instantaneous.

**P5 (Function and testimony).** The loop architecture is evolution's minimal solution to the one-body-under-novelty problem — online constraint satisfaction over a single global workspace using slow, noisy, cheap components. Control of that workspace requires a compressed self-model, whose caricatured representation of its own states ("intrinsic, private, ineffable") is the source of qualia-talk and of the hard problem's apparent intractability.

## Definition closure required before a compliance test

Author clarification (2026-10-25): the 100–1,000 ms range in P4 is a biological example of a **substrate-relative loop-timescale rule**, not a universal absolute duration. Effective loop times include integration and other dynamical delays, not just wire propagation. Evaluate the window relative to those dynamics; neither slowing this chip to biological speeds nor assigning an arbitrary long recording window is required or sufficient.

1. **State and boundary:** define rho for this classical noisy circuit: instantaneous voltages, a conditional probability distribution, a stationary ensemble, or another specified object. Enumerate included auxiliary states, environmental couplings and physical support circuits.
2. **Fields and interventions:** specify coarse variables, perturbing sources and their units. An operational response candidate is `G_R,ij(t,t') = delta E[x_i(t)] / delta u_j(t')`, with conditioning and perturbation regime declared. This is not by itself a definition of Phi_CLT. Specify what "dressed" means in the chosen stochastic model, and whether measured responses already include the relevant nonlinear feedback/noise corrections.
3. **Cycle functional:** specify whether cycles are simple, repeated walks, or field-theoretic diagrams; length/window limits, signs, integration measures, normalization and convergence. A spatial return must occur at a later time; a product requiring strictly retarded responses around a closed time ordering is not the intended positive-delay feedback path. A dressed response can already contain many physical paths, so multiplying dressed responses along graph edges needs a double-counting rule.
4. **Transported information:** choose the measure and conditioning, its units, and how it excludes redundant/common-input information. Response amplitude is not information transport. Define how correlations and interventions enter its estimator rather than identifying ordinary covariance with causation.
5. **Factorization:** define admissible partitions, the intervention or surrogate that removes inter-part coupling, preserved marginals/boundaries, objective and normalization. State what is minimized/maximized and how disconnected independent systems behave.
6. **Scale/extrema:** specify allowable coarse-grainings and their causal consistency, neighborhood for local extrema, maximum versus minimum, overlap/tie handling, and comparison across different subsystem sizes and time windows. Merely extending an observation window must not produce an uncontrolled score increase by counting repeated cycles.
7. **Temporal requirement:** define the admissible window relative to measured effective loop times and how to compare windows without duration-counting bias. The biological example is not a chip-speed requirement. Long trial duration alone does not establish a relevant loop timescale.
8. **Functional/testimony scope:** specify tests for constrained control under novelty, self-model compression and minimality. The current chip implements bounded telemetry, not language or qualia-talk. A control model cannot verify the evolutionary claim or the ontological identity by itself.
9. **Criticality:** P1–P5 do not yet define a numerical criticality condition or require each loop gain to equal one. Specify the relation between Phi_CLT, critical dynamics and regional gain regulation before treating a servo or bifurcation as compliance.

The reference to "17" is unresolved because that source requirement was not supplied. No empirical proof of P1 or of its asserted uniqueness is inferred from the absence of new forces.

## Consequences for the intended chip

- Physically recurrent perception/workspace/observer/schema paths and causal perturbation access are relevant to P1/P2/P5.
- The 32-site intervention and 96-channel hold system provides partial response observations, not all cycles or a whole-system Phi_CLT measurement.
- Existing partition controls support candidate factorization experiments. Their match to P3 depends on the factorization definition.
- P4 uses substrate-relative loop times, following the author's clarification; P5's testimony clause remains outside the bounded reporter's demonstrated abilities.
- Neither external field injection nor an endogenous field mechanism is required by P1 as written. CEMI is a separate theoretical requirement.
- Electrical precarity and a valence postulate do not occur in the supplied five postulates. Earlier review discussion of them is not part of this CLT definition.

## Required mathematical sanity tests

Before choosing circuit parameters to maximize Phi_CLT, define and test the functional on: an acyclic circuit; a single feedback loop; independent loops; weakly and strongly coupled loops; a redundant synchronized network; a delayed loop; and two alternative circuit realizations of the same observable dynamics. Check coordinate/unit changes, duration scaling, redundant-variable insertion, noise, nonlinear saturation and partial observability. These are tests of the proposed definition, not predetermined claims about which networks are conscious.

Background on the response/correlation distinction: [Fluctuation-Dissipation: Response Theory in Statistical Physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0719). This source does not endorse CLT or supply its missing functional.
